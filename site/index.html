Keep your Dock where it belongs.

Berth keeps the macOS Dock on the display you choose, without getting in the way of your pointer.

Download Read the guide
Dock berth

Choose a display. Berth handles the rest.

It blocks the Dock summon gesture on other outer edges and brings the Dock back when macOS moves it.

Your pointer stays free

Shared display edges remain open, so moving between screens still feels normal.

Your Dock comes back

Berth checks its position and summons it to the selected display when needed.

Your activity stays local

No keyboard monitoring, saved input, analytics, or network connection.

Ready in a minute.

Download the App directly or let Homebrew manage updates.
